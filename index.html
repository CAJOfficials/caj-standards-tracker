Important Note

Standards, qualifying periods, age categories and accepted courses can change. Swim Ontario had not released a 2026-27-specific OSC/OAG revision when this page was built, so the current published standards set is shown, alongside the previous season's Regional standards. Before entering a meet, confirm every requirement in the meet package and with your coach.

A Note for Swimmers

Remember: every swimmer’s journey is completely unique. While this tool is here to make tracking specific regional and provincial benchmarks easier, it is just a set of numbers.

For many of our Aquajets, swimming is about building lifelong friendships, staying active, mastering skills and simply having fun in the water. Whether you are chasing a specific cut, aiming to shave off a single second, or just competing for the love of the sport, your value to our team isn’t measured by a clock. Use this tool if it helps you set your own personal goals, but remember that progress comes in many forms — and enjoying the ride is the most important part of all.
